Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled \textbf{``Scale-Adaptive Piecewise-Quadratic Rectifiers for Rare-Event Genomic and Multi-Domain Screening: Offset-Scale Equivalence, Mechanistic Diagnostics, and Reproducible Computational Validation''} by Preecha Noiumkar and Thawatchai Chomsiri for consideration for publication in \textit{Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine Update}.

Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) are widely deployed in biomedical informatics for rare-event candidate screening, such as germline variant filtering in high-throughput sequencing read pileups. Under severe class imbalance ($10:1$ to $200:1$), classifiers are highly susceptible to silent majority-class collapse---achieving $>99\%$ overall accuracy while failing to detect any minority positive events ($\text{MCC} = 0.0000$). While piecewise-quadratic $C^1$-continuous rectifiers such as Smooth ReLU (SmeLU) and Smooth RectLU offer hardware-efficient alternatives to transcendental activations, asymmetric formulations anchored at $L = 0, W = 2$ suffer from severe optimization stagnation in multi-layer 2D architectures.

In this study, we provide a unified theoretical, diagnostic, and software framework that directly advances computational methods in biomedicine:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Exact Mechanistic Dissection \& Offset-Scale Equivalence:} We prove that $C^1$ smoothness itself does not cause collapse; rather, Smooth RectLU satisfies the exact algebraic identity $f_S(x; L, W) = \text{SmeLU}_{W/2}(x - L - W/2)$. Initializing at $L = 0, W = 2$ imposes an implicit negative bias offset ($\Delta b = -1$), a zero origin slope, and severe second-moment contraction ($\text{gain} = 0.0874$). Through $2\times 2$ factorial experiments, all-layer bias compensation ablations (Groups A--F), and backward Jacobian derivations, we disentangle initialization confounds from scale-width mismatch.
    \item \textbf{Parameter-Free Scale Adaptation ($\text{Symmetric } \sigma\text{-AS-RectLU}$):} We introduce Symmetric Per-Sample $\sigma$-AS-RectLU, which dynamically locks transition width to each sample's root-mean-square scale via stop-gradient locking ($W_l = \kappa \operatorname{stopgrad}(\hat{\sigma}_{\text{RMS}}), L_l = -W_l / 2$). This formulation restores degree-1 positive homogeneity, requires zero trainable affine parameters, and reduces GPU training time by $33\%$ compared to differentiable RMS scaling or explicit LayerNorm.
    \item \textbf{Rigorous Biomedical \& Variant-Calling Backbone Validation:} Using shortcut-controlled NIST Genome in a Bottle (GIAB GRCh38 v4.2.1, HG001--HG007) benchmarks, we evaluate 1D unit-$L_2$-normalized $k$-mer spectra and 2D BAM read-pileup tensors stratified across variant types (SNV vs.\ Small Indel), zygosity (Heterozygous $0/1$ vs.\ Homozygous $1/1$), and biological read-depth downsampling ($50\times \to 10\times$). Furthermore, on a 2D Hard-Negative Genomic Stress Benchmark containing strand-biased sequencing errors, chimeric reads, and homopolymer slippage, we benchmark our method against five handcrafted/classical ML baselines, two statistical pileup callers (GATK-style Diploid LLR and FreeBayes-style Strand-Aware callers), and clinical neural variant-caller backbones (\textbf{Clair3-Style PileupNet} and \textbf{DeepVariant-Style Multi-Scale InceptionNet}). Our results demonstrate that while uncompensated Smooth RectLU collapses in Clair3-style ($\text{MCC} = 0.0000$) and DeepVariant-style backbones ($\text{MCC} = 0.4478, 41.4\text{ FP/1k}$), Symmetric $\sigma$-AS-RectLU restores near-ceiling screening accuracy ($\text{MCC} = 0.9722$--$0.9877, \text{ECE} = 0.0036, 0.0\text{ FP/1k}$).
    \item \textbf{Reproducible Open-Source Software Package:} In alignment with the software and reproducibility scope of \textit{CMPB Update}, all datasets (\texttt{.npz}), per-seed evaluation logs (\texttt{.csv}), hardware profiling scripts, automated unit tests verifying mathematical identities, and a PEP~621 installable Python package (\texttt{pyproject.toml}) are publicly archived at \url{https://github.com/preecha-msu/rectlu-genomic-screening} (Release tag \texttt{v4.0.0}).
\end{enumerate}
